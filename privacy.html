Cupd.

privacy.

Draft for launch preparation. Review this against the final production setup before App Store submission.

Cupd. is a social coffee app. This policy explains the information Cupd. may collect, why it is used, and the choices available to users.

information you provide

This may include account information such as email address, username, display name and profile photo, as well as Cupd posts including photos, ratings, captions, café information, comments, reactions and friend connections.

location

If you choose to use nearby café features, Cupd. may request foreground location access to help find cafés near you. Location permission can be changed in your device settings.

how information is used

Information is used to provide account access, social features, coffee posts, maps, notifications, support and app security.

service providers

Cupd. may use third-party infrastructure providers for authentication, database hosting, file storage, push notifications, maps and app distribution. The final version of this policy should name the production providers actually in use at launch.

data retention and deletion

Users may request account deletion. Cupd. will provide an in-app deletion option or clear deletion instructions. Some data may be retained where required for security, legal or fraud-prevention reasons.

contact

For privacy questions, contact the support address listed on the support page.
