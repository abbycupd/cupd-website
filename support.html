Need a hand with Cupd.? For account help, technical problems, privacy requests, inappropriate content or anything else relating to Cupd., email support@cupd.co.uk.

Something broken?

Tell us what you were trying to do, what happened instead, your device/iOS version if known, and include a screenshot where useful. Please never send your password.

Forgot your password?

Select Forgot password? from the Cupd. login screen, enter the email associated with your account and follow the reset link sent to you.

Not receiving a reset email?

Check your spam or junk folder and make sure you entered the email associated with your account. If it still doesn't arrive, email us.

Delete your account

You can permanently delete your account from within Cupd. Account deletion removes your account and associated content from Cupd.'s active systems, subject to information we may need to retain for legal, security or technical reasons.

Photos, camera & location

Cupd. may ask for permission to use your camera, photo library and location. You can manage these permissions at any time through your device settings.

Notifications

You can manage Cupd. notification permissions through your device settings.

Report inappropriate content or behaviour

If you see harassment, threats, hate, explicit material, impersonation, spam, privacy violations or anything else that may breach our Community Guidelines, contact support@cupd.co.uk.

Please include the relevant username and enough information for us to identify the content.

Privacy

For questions about your personal information or to exercise a data protection right, email support@cupd.co.uk and read our Privacy Policy.

Legal

Use of Cupd. is subject to our Terms of Service.
