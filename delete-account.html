delete account.

Cupd. will provide an in-app account deletion option before public launch.

before launch

If you are part of the beta and need your account removed, contact support@cupd.co.uk from the email address associated with your Cupd. account.

what deletion will cover

The production deletion flow should remove or anonymise the account and associated personal data, subject to any limited retention required for security or legal reasons.

This page should be updated once the final in-app deletion flow is implemented.
