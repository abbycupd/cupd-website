The best coffee in Belfast city centre

coffee, captured. ☕️

Worth the trip in on its own. A couple of spots in the centre stand well above the usual chains.

Nica Nica

A tiny Italian artisan café tucked into the Great Northern Mall. Sicilian cannoli, real Italian coffee, and a warm, hidden-gem feel that's worth seeking out.

Trait

Specialty coffee and an in-house bakery, with the flagship on High Street right in the heart of the city centre. A reliably excellent cup, and a proper spot to sit and work.

Daisies

Skip the coffee here and go straight for the hot chocolate. It's award-winning, made with sustainably sourced cacao and topped with fresh marshmallows. A must if you've a sweet tooth.
