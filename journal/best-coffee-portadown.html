The best coffee in Portadown

coffee, captured. ☕️

A short hop down the road from Lurgan, and home to a couple of proper standouts.

Above All

Above the rest for a reason. Regulars talk about the owners as much as the coffee, and it's dog friendly too, so bring the four-legged one along.

Morning Buzz

A solid morning stop with a name that does exactly what it says on the tin.

Chimes Coffee House

Right in the centre of town and one of the most consistently recommended cafés in the area. Good coffee, generous tray-bakes, and outdoor seating when the weather allows.
